# Write a call to action with a reason to act now

People who ask more often get more yeses, and most marketing asks once or not
at all. The limit is goodwill: an ask with nothing around it teaches people to
stop listening. So ask often, and put real value between the asks.

## The formula

1. **Clear, not clever.** Say exactly what to do, verb first: "Start free",
   "Book a 20-minute call". No wordplay.
2. **The exact next step.** What happens when they click: which page opens,
   what they do first, what they get. Walk them one step, not the whole path.
3. **A reason to do it now.** Urgency, scarcity, or both when you have them.
   When you do not, any honest reason still beats no reason. A light reason is
   enough for a small ask like a free trial; a big ask like a sales call or a
   paid plan needs a reason that holds up.

## Truth rules for the reason

- Deadlines and limits must be real. No invented deadlines, fake seat counts,
  or countdowns that reset.
- A service business has real capacity. A small firm can only take a few new
  clients at a time, and buyers assume you are bigger than you are, so say the
  true number. A larger firm can start clients in weekly cohorts and state that
  limit.
- A self-serve product has no capacity limit, so borrow the buyer's own
  deadline: month-end reports, the client call this week, the quarterly
  review. A real offer window, like the length of a free trial, also counts.
- A true occasion works too: a launch, an anniversary, a season.

## Place the asks

- **Short ad**: one ask, at the end.
- **Organic post that already performs**: add a short ask at the end and run
  it as an ad. A long ask up front hurts the post.
- **Landing page, email sequence or content series**: ask again after each
  stretch of real value.
- Put the cost of saying yes beside the button when it is low, such as the
  length of a free trial and whether a card is needed.

## Review mode

For each ask in a draft, check four things: is it clear, is the next step
exact, is the reason true, and did real value come before it? Rewrite the ones
that fail.

## Output

The button label, the next-step line, the reason-now line, and where each ask
goes.
